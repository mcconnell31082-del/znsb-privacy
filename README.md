# ZNSB – The Bear Crew Privacy Policy

The Bears are fictional bots operated by the ZNSB server owner.

We process Discord account details, relevant messages and operational records to run the community. AI processing takes place locally. AI chat transcripts are not saved. Conversation context expires after 90 seconds and Hugh's limited HR metadata after 30 days. Operational records and backups may persist.

We do not sell personal data or use it to train AI. Discord's own privacy policy also applies.

For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests, use the contact button in the server's privacy-and-safety channel.

The Bears do not provide real HR or counselling services.
